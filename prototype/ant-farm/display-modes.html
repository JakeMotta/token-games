Ant Farm · ways to show it on a Mac

The colony here is a demo: one live farm, the real prototype's simulation and drawing, fed pretend tokens on a timer so it keeps moving. In the game only real Claude Code use feeds it, and there are no feed buttons.

All modes side by side

Recommendation

Best three, in this order:
  1. Notch island (with the menu-bar centre pill on Macs and displays without a notch) as the farm's home. It is always there, takes no desktop space, and a glance at the notch shows the colony is alive. Hover to peek, click for the big view.
  2. Click-to-expand window, which is the overlay you already have, reachable from the island's Pop out. Keep it for when you want to watch the colony while you work.
  3. Claude Code status line as a cheap extra: one line in the terminal where the tokens are being spent, no window at all.

The combined design (tab ★) puts these together: the farm lives in the notch, the bigger overlay is one click away, and a setting picks the home (Notch · Menu bar · Corner orb) for people who want something else. The menu-bar strip and the orb are the runners-up: the strip is the easiest to build and works everywhere; the orb is the friendliest on Windows. The wallpaper and the Dock icon are charming but you rarely see them, and the ground line is fun but always costs a strip of screen.
