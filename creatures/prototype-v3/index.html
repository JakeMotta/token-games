Style:

Seen from above

  • The skeleton is version 2's chain: each joint follows the one in front at a fixed distance and may bend only so far.
  • The body is a Catmull-Rom curve through points at each joint's radius, left and right of the spine, closed round the head and the tail tip.
  • Each leg is two bones from the hip to a planted foot out to the side; the knee bends outward. A foot stays put until it falls too far behind, then steps.
  • The shadow is the same outline moved away from the sun, longer and darker when the sun is low.
  • Turn on Skeleton to see the joints, their radii, the bend limits, the step circles and which feet are planted.
