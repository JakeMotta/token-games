      What the debug drawings show

      • Bones B. White lines: each leg's bones; v1's stiff spine (dashed); v2/v3's chain, every link the same length.
      • Joints & hips J. Pink squares: hips. Dots: chain joints; the cyan one is the head, the only part that is steered. Pink cross: where v1 pivots.
      • Body outline O. v1: the rings its body is built from. v2/v3: how wide the body is at each joint, and purple wedges for how far each link may bend (red: at the limit).
      • Feet & steps F. Green: planted. Orange: in the air (v1: swinging to the orange cross). Gold dashed circles (v2/v3): where a foot wants to be; outside it, it steps. A gold ring opens where a step starts.
      • Gait meters G. v1's gait clock: one ring per leg, green while that foot is down; the hand sweeps and the feet obey. v2/v3's "how far behind" bars: each fills as a foot falls behind and it steps at the line.
      • Trails T. Yellow: the path the body took. Dots: footprints, blue left, pink right.
      • Path & target P. Dashed: the path to follow. Red cross: the target.
      • Labels L. Names on the drawings ("head (steered)", the meters).
      • Shadow S. The creature's own cast shadow (part of the art, off by default here so only the body shows).
      • Floor grid R. Lines a quarter of a body length apart, to judge sliding feet against.
