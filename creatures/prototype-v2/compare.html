What is different here

    Numbers are measured live from the run. "By eye" is written down from watching each scenario. Hover a number for what it measures.

    What you are looking at

    • Blue circles. v1: the rings its body is built from, on a stiff spine. v2/v3: each chain joint and how wide the body is there.
    • White line with dots. v2/v3: the chain. Every link keeps the same length; each joint is dragged after the one in front.
    • Cyan arrow. v2/v3: the head, the only part that is steered. The rest follows.
    • Purple wedges. v2/v3: how far each link may bend from the one before. Red means it is at the limit.
    • Pink squares. Hips, where each leg hangs from the body.
    • Green feet. On the ground (planted). Orange: in the air, swinging to the orange cross (v1).
    • Dashed gold circles. v2/v3: where each foot wants to be. When a foot falls outside its circle (a stride behind) it steps. A gold ring opens where a step starts.
    • Gait clock (v1, top right): one ring per leg; green is when that foot is down. The hand sweeps round and the feet obey it.
    • "How far behind" bars (v2/v3, top right): no clock; each bar fills as a foot falls behind. At the dashed line it steps.
    • Trails. Yellow: the path the body took. Dots: footprints, blue left, pink right.
    • Dashed white. The path to follow. Red cross: the target.
